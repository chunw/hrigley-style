Artist Statement

CHUN WANG · NEW YORK

I run my home as my own private museum. It started with watercolor sketches, and my interest in the small trinkets scattered around the house has only grown since. These objects, sometimes a little grotesque, are pieces I've salvaged from all over for my collection. I often rearrange and combine them into new scenes where the imagination can play. I believe everyday objects can also give viewers a fresh visual experience and a real emotional response.

我把家当作自己的“私人博物馆”来经营。从水彩速写开始，我对散落家中的小摆设的兴趣愈发增长。这些有时带有怪诞气息的物件，是我从各处打捞的馆藏，也会时常被我排列组合，形成新的景观现场，供想象力嬉戏。我相信日常小物也可以为观者带来新鲜的视觉体验与心理感受。
